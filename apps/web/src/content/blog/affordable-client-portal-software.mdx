Client portal software ranges from free to several hundred dollars a month. The expensive end is usually priced for enterprise teams with compliance requirements and dedicated IT resources. The affordable end — under $50/month — covers the needs of most small agencies, freelancers, and small businesses perfectly well.

Here's what's available and what you actually get for the money.

## Free client portal software

### Salkaro Portal (free tier)

Salkaro Portal's free tier gives you one portal, connected to Monday.com or Linear, with no time limit and no credit card required. For a freelancer or small agency managing one main client project at a time, this is a complete solution at zero cost.

What you get on the free tier:
- 1 active portal
- Real-time sync with Monday.com or Linear
- All three access methods (open link, access code, email OTP)
- Unlimited client views

What's on paid plans:
- Multiple portals
- Custom branding (logo, colours, badge removal)
- Larger team sizes

**Best for:** Freelancers, solo consultants, agencies testing the tool before committing

---

### Notion (free personal plan)

A shared Notion page is free and requires no new software if you already use Notion. It's not a purpose-built portal — no PM sync, no branded experience — but for very basic deliverable sharing with one or two clients, the price (zero) is hard to argue with.

**Best for:** Freelancers with minimal needs who already use Notion

---

## Under $25/month

### Salkaro Portal Pro

The Pro plan unlocks unlimited portals, custom branding, and larger team sizes. For agencies managing multiple clients, this is where the real value lives — and the price reflects a focused tool rather than a bloated suite.

**Best for:** Growing agencies with multiple active clients who want professional branding

---

## Under $50/month

### HoneyBook Starter

HoneyBook bundles client portal features with contracts, invoicing, and scheduling for $16/month. If you need more than just a portal — particularly invoicing and proposals — this is one of the more affordable all-in-one options.

**Best for:** Freelancers and small service businesses needing portal + contracts + invoicing

**Note:** The portal features are less sophisticated than a dedicated portal tool; it's a bundle play

---

## $50-$100/month

### SuiteDash

At $19/month for the base plan (scaling up for more features), SuiteDash offers white-label portal, CRM, invoicing, and project management. The value calculation depends on how many of those features you actually use — if you just need a portal, it's overkill at this price.

**Best for:** Agencies wanting a full business suite at a relatively low entry price

---

## The real cost calculation

Monthly software cost is only part of the equation. Also consider:

**Time cost of manual alternatives**
If you're currently writing status update emails for 10 clients every week, that's potentially 2-3 hours per week. At any reasonable hourly rate, that time cost dwarfs the monthly cost of portal software.

**Churn prevention value**
Clients who feel informed and well-served stay longer. Even one retained client per year more than pays for a year of portal software.

**Pitch differentiation value**
"We'll set up a dedicated project portal for your account" is a differentiator in a competitive pitch. Hard to put an exact number on it, but it wins work.

## Recommended starting point

For most teams evaluating affordable portal software, the right starting point is Salkaro Portal's free tier. It requires no payment, connects to Monday.com or Linear in minutes, and gives you a live portal you can share with a client today.

If you manage more than one active client project, the Pro plan's unlimited portals and custom branding are worth the upgrade.

Start free. Upgrade when the volume justifies it.
